# school_india

Indian School ERP Custom Application.
Supports:
- Indian Academic Classes & Sections (Nursery to Grade 12)
- CBSE, ICSE, and Telangana State Board Exam Schemes (FA/SA/PT/CCE)
- Government Identifiers (UDISE+, PEN, APAAR) & Data Health Engine
- Sibling Discounts, RTE Concessions & Installment Plans
- Configurable School Certificates (TC, Bonafide, Conduct)
- Transport & Route Management
